LabourLink – Privacy Policy

Aakhri Update: 2024

LabourLink ("ham", "hamari app") aap ki privacy ki izzat karta hai. Ye policy batati hai k ham aap ka data kaise use karte hain.

1. Ham Kaunsa Data Lete Hain?

  • Mobile Number: Login aur account verification k liye (OTP).
  • Naam aur CNIC Number: Dono taraf (Mazdoor aur Employer) ke trust k liye.
  • GPS Location: Sirf jab aap "Available" ON karte hain ya kaam ki location set karte hain – taake qareeb ki jobs mil sakein.
  • Job aur Chat Records: Dono taraf ke faide aur disputes solve karne k liye.
  • Wallet aur Payment Records: Mazdoori aur commission hisaab k liye.

2. Data Ka Use Kis Liye Hota Hai?

  • Qareeb k mazdoor aur employers ko connect karne k liye
  • Escrow (mehfooz payment) system chalane k liye
  • Complaints/disputes ka faisla karne k liye
  • App behtar banane k liye

3. Data Kisi Ko Bechte Nahi

Ham aap ka personal data kisi third party ko bechte nahi hain. Data sirf app chalane k liye use hota hai. Firebase (Google) hamara database partner hai – unki apni security hoti hai.

4. Location Ki Detail

Aap ki location sirf tab share hoti hai jab zaroori ho (job match ya kaam ki jagah). Aap "Available" OFF kar ke location sharing band kar sakte hain.

5. Account Delete Karna

Aap kabhi bhi app mein Profile → Delete Account se apna account delete kar sakte hain. Is se aap ki profile aur personal details mit jati hain. Financial ledger records (paisay ka hisaab) legal zaroorat k liye mehfooz rakhe ja sakte hain.

6. Security

Ham aap ka data Firebase ke encrypted servers par rakhte hain. Lekin internet par 100% security koi guarantee nahi – is liye apna OTP kabhi kisi se share na karein.

7. Bachon K Liye Nahi

Ye app 18 saal se kam umar k logon k liye nahi hai.

8. Tabdeelian

Ye policy waqt k sath update ho sakti hai. Bari tabdeeli par app mein aagah kiya jaye ga.
