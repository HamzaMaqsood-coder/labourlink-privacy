LabourLink – Terms & Conditions

Aakhri Update: 2024

LabourLink use karne ka matlab hai aap in sharton (Terms) se ittefaq karte hain. Agar ittefaq nahi to app use na karein.

1. App Kya Hai?

LabourLink ek platform hai jo mazdoor aur employers ko connect karta hai. Ham sirf connection aur payment protection (Escrow) ka zariya hain – ham khud kisi ko kaam par nahi rakhte aur na kaam ki guarantee dete hain.

2. Commission (Service Fee)

  • Mazdoori ki tay hui raqam par 10% service fee mazdoor ki taraf se li jati hai.
  • Complaint/dispute ki soorat mein refund par bhi 10% service fee lage gi (jo admin ka faisla ho ga – full refund ya split dono cases mein).
  • Fee ki raqam waqt k sath tabdeel ho sakti hai – tabdeeli se pehle app mein aagah kiya jaye ga.

3. Escrow (Paisa Hold Karne Ka System)

  • Employer mazdoori app mein hold karta hai – ye mazdoor ki hifazat hai.
  • Paisa sirf tab release hota hai jab DONO taraf kaam mukammal confirm karein.
  • Dispute hone par paisa FREEZE rehta hai jab tak admin faisla na kare.
  • Admin ka faisla (full pay / refund / split) dono taraf par manzoor hoga.

4. Cancellation Rules

  • Escrow se pehle: Dono taraf deal cancel kar sakte hain (koi paisa nahi kata jata).
  • Escrow ke baad: Cancel nahi – sirf Complaint system k through (admin faisla kare ga).
  • Bar bar cancel karne par account warning/block ho sakta hai.

5. Dono Taraf Ki Zimmedari

  • Sahi CNIC aur sahi information dena zaroori hai – fake information par permanent ban hoga.
  • App k bahar deal karna (platform bypass) mana hai – is par account block ho sakta hai.
  • Badtameezi, dhoka, ya fake kaam ki complaint par admin faisla kare ga – uska faisla final hoga.
  • Number sirf apna ho – doosre ka number use karna mana hai.

6. Payments (Demo Phase)

Abhi app demo mode mein hai – escrow records system mein hote hain. Real payment integration (JazzCash/Easypaisa) launch par aaye gi. Demo phase mein koi real paisa transfer nahi hota.

7. Hamari Zimmedari Ki Hadd

  • Ham kaam ki quality, waqt par aana, ya kaam k doran hone wale nuqsan ki zimmedari nahi lete – ye dono taraf ki apni zimmedari hai.
  • Internet/service outage ki wajah se hui pareshani ki zimmedari nahi.
  • Users k darmiyan kisi bhi jhagre ka faisla admin kare ga, lekin legal disputes dono taraf ki apni zimmedari hain.

8. Account Delete / Block

Ham kisi bhi account ko rules torne par block/delete kar sakte hain. Aap kabhi bhi Profile → Delete Account se apna account delete kar sakte hain. Financial ledger records legal zaroorat k liye mehfooz reh sakte hain.

Ye Terms waqt k sath update ho sakte hain. Bari tabdeeli par app mein aagah kiya jaye ga.
